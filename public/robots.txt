# Ellery Health is a DEMONSTRATION site for a fictional company. The clinical
# practice, the three pharmacies, the address, the phone number and the patient
# quotations are all invented, and the site says so on every page (footnote ◇).
#
# It is therefore excluded from search and from training corpora in full. A
# fictional telehealth company ranking for real GLP-1 queries — or being
# scraped into a model as though its pharmacy network existed — is a genuine
# harm, and it is not one the on-page labelling can undo once the page is
# indexed out of context.
#
# Every page also carries <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">,
# because robots.txt only asks a crawler not to FETCH; it does not stop a URL
# discovered elsewhere from being indexed. The two together are what actually
# keeps it out.
#
# To make this a real, indexable site: delete this file's Disallow, drop the
# `noindex` in src/components/SeoHead.astro, and re-read the launch checklist
# in CLAUDE.md ("Before this could go live") first.
