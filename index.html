Questions

The ones that come up first

Every question we get asked
01

Is Ellery a pharmacy?

No, and it is not a medical practice either. Ellery builds and runs the intake, moves your information to the right party, handles payment, tracks the order and answers the phone. The clinical decision belongs to an independently licensed clinician, and the medication is dispensed by a state-licensed pharmacy. Neither works for Ellery.

02

Then who decides whether I qualify?

A clinician licensed in your state, affiliated with an independent professional practice. They read your intake, they may ask follow-up questions, and they decide. Ellery cannot see a way to influence that decision, because there is not one — the thresholds they apply are published on the pricing page so you can check yourself against them first.

03

What happens if I am not approved?

The review fee is refunded in full, automatically, to the card you used. You are not charged for the medication, because no prescription was written. You will be told which part of the intake led to the decision, and whether a different program might be reviewed instead.

04

Is the medication FDA-approved?

The compounded preparations are not. They are made by a state-licensed pharmacy against a prescription for one person, under Section 503A or 503B, and the FDA does not review or approve them for safety, effectiveness or quality. FDA-approved brand-name products exist for semaglutide and tirzepatide, a clinician may recommend one instead, and their pharmacy price is published on the metabolic program page rather than hidden behind a call.

05

How can the administration fee be the whole fee?

Because it is the only thing Ellery sells. The clinician bills for the review, the pharmacy bills for the medication and the fulfilment, and Ellery bills a fixed amount per order for the coordination. Ellery does not mark the medication up, and it is not paid more when a more expensive drug is prescribed — which is also the arrangement the rules require.

06

Is this available where I live?

In most places, but not everywhere, and we would rather say so here than at checkout. The first question in the intake is your state, and it will tell you immediately if the platform is not yet operating there. Nothing else is asked, and nothing is charged, if the answer is no.
